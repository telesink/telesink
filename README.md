# Telesink Event Tracking

**Status: WIP**

## Know what your product is doing. Right now.

Telesink is an event tracking tool that acts as an informational heartbeat for
your business — so you can literally see what’s happening _right now_.

It’s a cleaner, purpose-built replacement for those read-only bots in Slack,
Discord, or Telegram that were never meant to do this job.

Hook up your projects and watch live **business** events stream in:

- People are signing up
- Payments are flowing
- MRR is ticking up
- New trials are starting
- Users are completing onboarding
- Form submissions are coming in
- Subscribers are renewing
- AI responses are being generated
- Orders are being placed
- Support tickets are being resolved
- Campaigns are generating leads
- …and dozens more.

**Unlike analytics or monitoring tools that focus on historical data, developer
metrics, logs, or server stats, Telesink is built for everyone — not just
developers.**

**Telesink is open source and free to self-host.**
There’s also a paid SaaS version to support ongoing development.

### Who it's for

Telesink gives real-time visibility to any team that needs to stay on top of
what’s happening in the business:

- **Product & Growth teams** – tracking user journeys, feature adoption, and
  onboarding flow
- **Customer Success & Support** – seeing engagement, ticket resolution, and
  churn signals live
- **Marketing teams** – monitoring campaign performance and lead generation in
  real time
- **Sales teams** – watching pipeline movement and key conversions as they
  happen
- **Business leaders & executives** – getting an instant pulse on revenue, MRR,
  and overall health
- **Revenue Operations & Finance** – tracking payments, renewals, and financial
  metrics live
- **Agencies & consultants** – managing multiple client projects and dashboards
  in one place
- **E-commerce and SaaS companies** – needing operational visibility into
  orders, checkouts, and subscriptions
- **AI product teams & LLM operators** – monitoring AI feature usage, prompt
  volume, token consumption, agent completions, and generative feature adoption
  in real time
- **AI agencies** – overseeing multiple client AI tools and campaigns from a
  single live dashboard
