# Telesink Event Tracking

**Status: WIP**

## Know what your product is doing. Right now.

Telesink is an event tracking tool that acts as an informational heartbeat for
your business — so you can literally see what’s happening _right now_.

It’s a cleaner, purpose-built replacement for those read-only bots in Slack,
Discord, or Telegram that were never meant to do this job.

Hook up your projects and watch live events stream in:

- People are signing up
- Payments are flowing
- MRR is ticking up
- New trials are starting
- Users are completing onboarding
- Form submissions are coming in
- Subscribers are renewing
- …and dozens more.

Unlike traditional analytics tools that bury you in historical data, Telesink is
simple by design — a straightforward, all-in-one dashboard built for everyone,
not just developers.

**Telesink is open source and free to self-host.**
There’s also a paid [SaaS version](https://telesink.com) to keep development alive.
